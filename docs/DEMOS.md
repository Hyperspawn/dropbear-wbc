# Dropbear demo gallery (motion tracking, Isaac Lab + Newton sim2sim)

What Dropbear can show today in simulation with the Unitree H1/G1 motion-tracking stack (BeyondMimic port,
`docs/CONTRACTS.md` 5). One policy per clip, trained in Isaac Lab 2.2 / PhysX on the contract plant (the user's USD
`45586414` with the in-memory spawn fixes of CONTRACTS 0.1-0.3), evaluated at 32/4 solver iterations, rendered with the
clean demo renderer (CONTRACTS 5.3 "Clean render"), and replayed in Newton (MuJoCo C on the CPU) through the SDK bridge.

Progress log and evidence: `logs/demo_eval/PROGRESS.md`. Media: `logs/demo_eval/media/`. All numbers below were measured
by a command whose log is cited; "VERIFIED" means that run exists, "UNVERIFIED" means it was not run. Finite simulation
is not success: each row says what the robot actually does, checked by eye on the rendered frames.

## 2. What the videos show (visual check of the rendered frames)

### 2.1 dance_102 (model_2300)

- The robot stays upright for the whole 29.1 s clip, knees bent, feet about shoulder width apart or wider.
- **Upper body and turns follow the choreography**: the whole body turns about 45-90 deg around 17.7 s and 24.7 s like
  the reference; the right arm swings out sideways to shoulder height (about 9.7-15.2 s and 22.2 s); a raised bent arm
  around 20 s. The arm poses are straighter than the reference (less elbow bend).
- **The legs are simplified**: where the reference lifts or crosses a leg and both "stance" feet float 5-14 cm above
  the floor (visible in the reference feet video: detached shadows at about 9.7, 12.7 and 26 s), the policy keeps both
  soles flat on the floor and shifts its weight instead. It repositions its feet between poses; it does not reproduce
  the floating steps (it physically cannot). This is also why the anchor position error (0.23 m) is large while the
  body/joint errors are moderate: the root does not travel along the reference path.
- The 3.02 rad RH_yaw reference jump at 22.1 s causes no visible jerk or fall (the policy smooths it).
- This is an exploratory result on a rejected reference, not a faithful replay of the G1 dance.

### 2.2 wave_right v1 (model_743)

The robot stands still on planted feet, raises the right arm forward-sideways to about shoulder height with a slight
elbow bend, sweeps it back and forth (the wave, about 2-8 s) and lowers it again by 9.5 s; the left arm and the legs
stay still. Isaac (all three evaluation modes) and Newton both complete the clip.

### 2.3 wave_right_v2 (model_1179): FAILED

The reference raises the right upper arm 15 deg above horizontal, 40 deg right of forward, with the forearm VERTICAL
(hand at 1.655 m, beside the head) and waves at 1.5 Hz (shoulder yaw +-30 deg, wrist roll +-15 deg).

- **At 32/4 (the evaluation solver)** the policy lifts the arm straight out forward-sideways at shoulder height and never
  flexes the forearm up; the right-hand height error passes the 0.25 m `ee_body_pos` limit at 3.64 s, where the
  full-amplitude wave starts, and every episode ends there (the video restarts from frame 0 three times).
- **At 8/4 (the training solver)** the same checkpoint flexes the elbow and holds the hand at about head height, but IN
  FRONT of the face (upper arm forward, "salute" pose) instead of beside the head, and waves from there; 0 terminations,
  joint error 1.46 rad (dominated by the shoulder: in Newton RH_yaw rms 1.32 rad).
- Diagnosis: the policy sits at the edge of the termination limit and its elbow-up behaviour does not transfer from the
  8/4 training solver to the 32/4 evaluation solver (closure gaps 1.16 mm vs 0.48 mm max; a plausible, UNVERIFIED cause
  is that it relies on the softer 8/4 closures of the elbow four-bar). The warm start from the v1 policy plus 436
  iterations (budget 45 min) did not find the reference arm configuration. Newton (2 ms, stiff closures) sits in
  between: no terminations in the runner, right hand max 1.54 m vs 1.65 m.

## 4. Renderer notes

- Clean renderer: `source/dropbear_wbc/tasks/tracking/demo_render.py` (`apply_clean_render`, `DemoRecorder`), used by
  `scripts/play.py --clean_video` and `scripts/render_reference.py`. No debug markers, white dome + key light.
- **Framing** (fixed 2026-09-24 16:20): the first renders (18 mm lens) cropped head and feet; projecting the reference
  bodies into those frames gave an effective focal length ~1.3-1.5x the nominal one (cause not identified). Now 12 mm,
  track camera 4.4 m (eye 1.55 m, look-at 1.0 m), fixed front camera at max(5 m, root extent + 4 m); the low feet camera
  keeps 18 mm. The pre-fix renders `wave_right_policy_*.mp4` / `wave_right_v2_reference_*.mp4` (15:23-15:24) are kept in
  `logs/demo_eval/media/superseded/`. The fixed front camera is a wide shot (robot small but always fully in frame); the
  track camera is the main view.
- **Blank-frame glitch** (found 2026-09-24 21:30): the RTX `rgb` annotator sometimes returns a full-size but blank frame
  (all black, or uniform washed-out grey without floor or robot): 25-61 % of the frames in two play renders, while the
  summary still said `empty_frames: 0`. `DemoRecorder` now checks every frame (`frame_is_valid`: colour + contrast),
  re-renders (no physics step) until valid, and reports `retried_frames` / `invalid_frames` per camera. That was NOT
  enough (22:04 wave v1 play render: still 124-149 of 500 frames blank per camera after 8 retries). Every kinematic
  replay was clean, so policy videos are now rendered from the recorded rollout: `scripts/play.py --record_rollout`
  saves env 0's simulated state per policy step (contract NPZ under `logs/demo_eval/rollouts/`, `meta.not_a_reference`),
  `scripts/render_reference.py` renders it without stepping physics, and `tools/render_policy_clean.py` chains both and
  checks every frame (`<tag>_frame_check.json`). The picture is the simulated state; only the rendering differs. The
  glitch's cause is not identified. Check any mp4 before publishing it (glitch frames: saturation < 1.3, real ones
  10-18; `frame_is_valid`, test `tests/test_demo_render_frames.py`).
- The grid floor renders blue (Isaac default grid material); the grey tint in `apply_clean_render` has no visible effect.

## 5. Open issues

1. dance_102 reference fails the contact gate and the motor-dynamics gate (floating stance feet, 77.5 rad/s spikes, a
   branch flip at 22.1 s). A faithful dance needs foot-contact-aware retargeting (the foot_contact track) and a re-train.
2. dance_102 root drift: anchor error 0.23-0.30 m in Isaac, root 0.31 m mean off the reference path in Newton.
3. wave_right_v2 policy failed at 32/4 and uses the wrong arm configuration at 8/4. Next: train at 16/4 or 32/4 (or
   measure the 8/4-vs-32/4 gap of the elbow four-bar), train from scratch or longer, or stage the target (curriculum on
   the hand height); check that the elbow four-bar can hold the vertical-forearm pose statically.
4. Sim2sim end of clip: after the policy hands over to the PD hold at motion end, the robot falls about 1 s later in
   Newton (dance 1.07 s, wave v2 0.97 s, wave v1 0.96 s). The sim2sim report's `fall` field only covers the runner
   window, which ends before these falls; `verify_sim2sim_trace.py` reports them. A deploy needs a policy-held stand or
   a stand policy after the clip.
5. Sim2sim is CPU MuJoCo C with `--diag-eq-solref 0.004 1` (stiffer closures); MuJoCo Warp (GPU) has no eq_solref
   override yet.
6. The renderer's effective focal length and the blank-frame glitch are not understood, only worked around.
7. Solver sensitivity: policies are trained at 8/4 and evaluated at 32/4 (CONTRACTS 5); dance and wave v1 transfer,
   wave v2 does not. An 8/4 check belongs in every evaluation.
